I am a graduate student in Electrical Engineering at the University of Buffalo, specializing in power electronics and energy systems.

Prior to my graduate studies, I worked as a Software Engineer at Quest Global, where I developed high-quality applications and collaborated with cross-functional teams to solve complex, real-world problems.

My technical interests lie in renewable energy systems and power electronics. I am particularly interested in designing and optimizing energy efficient systems and sustainable infrastructure. I am skilled in MATLAB, JavaScript and Python, and hoping to contribute to innovative projects while continuing to grow as an engineer.

# Hobbies

## Programming

From a young age, I was fascinated by the world of computers and software. I always wondered how everything from websites, mobile apps and the Internet itself was built and how they function. Over the years, I learnt how to do so, from making simple HTML forms to working on large-scale commercial web applications before coming to UB.

I have been programming as a hobby since 2022, and I am a big supporter of open-source GitHub projects. I find them to be more reliable and improvable as software tools due to the community effort in maintaining standards and delivering a good user experience.

## Reading

I find books to be one of the best ways to gain knowledge, as well as experience the best stories. The first books I read were the Harry Potter books back in middle school. Since then, I have been reading on and off on my Kindle, as well as listening to audiobooks.

## Listening to music

It is commonly said that music is the sound of the soul, and I am inclined to agree. Music has helped me through tough times and made me feel emotions I never thought I was able to. Over the past decade, I have discovered several artists over diverse genres that I keep listening to while working or doing chores.

## Watching movies

The first time I actually went to the theatre out of interest (and not because my family or friends forced me to), was in 2023 to watch Oppenheimer, as it came out just after I started my job at Quest Global. Since then, I have watched movies more frequently than I used to.

## Gaming

Even though many people don't think gaming is a worthwhile hobby, I feel video games seamlessly combine the art of music and movies in one beautiful package. During the pandemic, I was able to play 'Among Us' with my friends, allowing us to hang out even though we couldn't see each other in person. One of the best experiences in this hobby was playing through the single-player game 'Red Dead Redemption 2', which made me realize that the effort the creators put into their work is not to be overlooked.
